II. Key Layout & Aesthetic Principles

The layout strictly adheres to the TwinNU design principles, ensuring a premium, focused user experience.
Minimalism & Focus: Extensive use of whitespace/darkspace. Content blocks are centered and concise. The Frosted Glass effect is reserved only for overlay elements (Navbar, Modals) to maintain a clean background view.
Aesthetic Gimmick: Frosted Glass: The bg-black/40 (Dark Mode) or bg-white/70 (Light Mode) transparent background combined with backdrop-filter: blur(12px) creates a subtle, high-end effect on the Fixed Navbar and Feature Cards.
Aesthetic Gimmick: Neon Accent: The Neon Accent color (#00FFFF) provides the primary visual pop. It is exclusively used for the H1 Headline and the Primary CTA Button ("Join the Pilot") to enforce a clear Information Hierarchy.
Animation Pace: All animations (Framer Motion scroll reveals, text glides) are intentionally slow (300-500ms duration) to reinforce the "Slow Social" brand concept.
III. Reusable Component Usage

Component reusability is managed using class-variance-authority (cva) and Shadcn/ui primitives to ensure a consistent look and easy state management (e.g., 'coming soon').
